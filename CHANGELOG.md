# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased): testers' preview

The first build for other people's devices (macOS, Android, the keeper on Linux). Written up for testers in R1-12.

- From this version on, wire protocols, signing domains and on-disk files are kept compatible: see
  [docs/notes/formats.md](docs/notes/formats.md) and "Changing a format" in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- On-disk layout 2: data from earlier development builds is upgraded on first start (a copy of the small files is kept
  under `backups/`).
- Settings → About shows the version, the build and where the source is; the third-party licence notices ship with
  the app and the keeper (`shoebox-keeper licences`), generated by `scripts/licences.py`.
- Security alerts (a new admin device, a replaced recovery phrase) reach you with the app closed: a notification on
  Android and on the desktop, and `shoebox-keeper status` / `alerts` on the keeper.
- Recently Deleted can be emptied without waiting 30 days: *Delete N now* for the selection or *Delete all now*, on
  every device at once (A-103).
- Android: backup takes only camera photos by default (`DCIM`, not screenshots, downloads or other apps' pictures).
  *Back up camera roll → Choose folders* lists every folder with photos in it to pick from, or every folder (A-102).
- Android: opening the app while a background backup is running no longer closes the app; the background run stops
  and hands over. Debug builds put the background run's log tail in its report (`adb logcat -s ShoeboxBurst`).
- Long videos play on Android (they never started: the WebView re-applies byte ranges to the app's own URL scheme,
  so videos now come from a loopback server inside the app, A-098), video pieces are 4 MB instead of the whole file,
  and reading them is about 20 times faster. A video the device cannot play says so instead of offering "Get
  original" again.
- Videos play in the viewer on every device: while waiting it loaded the video's "preview" (the video itself, as
  videos have no still preview yet) as an image, and that image failing removed the video that had started
  playing. The viewer now shows the poster frame or nothing while waiting, and only the element showing the original
  can report it unreadable. Shared-album grids no longer load whole videos as thumbnails.
- Large originals (8 MB and up) come from all the devices that hold them at once, in pieces, each verified as it
  arrives; a device that cannot help is skipped (A-099). On home Wi-Fi a phone got 128 MB from a Mac and a Linux keeper
  together in about 5 s, against 6.5 s from the Mac alone and 12 s from the keeper alone.
- Devices is one list: each device shows how it is connected, what it keeps (originals and previews, one shared
  scale) and what it is sending or getting right now. A card on top says what is moving, how far along and how long
  is left, or why it is waiting, and opens into this device's queue. Roles and removing are in a ⋯ menu on the
  desktop and a sheet on the phone (mockup 07).
- Phones and computers that are no keeper do not keep a preview next to an original they hold (JPEG, PNG): the
  viewer shows the original. A preview goes once a keeper holds it, and comes back before the original is freed. On
  the test library previews were about a quarter of the photos' size (A-100).
- Thumbnails and previews are 13% smaller and encoded 2.6 times as fast (4:2:0 JPEG from `jpeg-encoder`, A-101).
- The app's icon (the two linked prints) replaces the 📦 emoji everywhere: the loading screen, the sidebar, first run
  and the website, which also uses it as its tab icon.
- The timeline has a date scroller on the right: a pill handle that shows while you scroll or hover, year labels where
  each year starts and a bubble with the month while you drag.
- The app feels less like a web page: no text selection by dragging, no I-beam cursor, no dragging images out, no tap
  flash or page bounce, and no webview right-click menu in release builds. Text fields and copyable values still
  select.
- Selecting: a circle on each photo and by each date (on hover with a mouse), the date selects its day while
  selecting, and a small floating bar of glyphs that no longer pushes the grid down (on a phone a long-press then
  selected the photo above). A long-press drag selects the whole run and scrolls at the top and bottom edges.
- Share: selected photos go into one of your shared albums (or a new one), or to other apps through the system share
  sheet on Android and the share picker on macOS; originals not on the device are fetched first.
- Settings → Leave this library: the device stops syncing and starts over at first run, to create a new library or
  join another. Its data folder is set aside next to itself (`….left-<library>-<time>`), not deleted.
- The six-digit pairing code shows wherever you are in the app: with the "Add a device" sheet closed (the code
  copied out of it), it had nowhere to appear and the other device waited until the pairing timed out.
- Restoring with the recovery phrase asks what the device does (keeper, companion, viewer), as creating a library
  does: a phone restored as a keeper and fetched every original.
- `shoebox-keeper pair --name` is the keeper's name from then on; it was used only during pairing, and the keeper
  showed as "keeper" afterwards.
- The app and the keeper tell you when a newer version is out (Settings → About → Check for new versions; the keeper's
  `status`). Nothing is installed automatically.
- A phone keeps one copy of each photo: gallery photos are read where they are, never copied into Shoebox, and a photo
  deleted in the gallery is deleted from the library too (Recently Deleted on every device). Copies made by earlier
  builds are replaced by references on first start. Many photos disappearing at once ask first.
- The middle role is now **Companion** (it was "Phone"): it keeps the full-size photos taken in a recent window (90 days
  by default; off, 30 days, 90 days or 1 year in Settings), your favourites (a toggle, on by default) and everything
  pinned; older originals live on keepers and are fetched when opened. Free-up leaves what the rules keep. Older
  settings that say "phone" still load. Fixed on the way: a restart forgot pinned photos, so free-up could release one.
- The keeper's command line can do what an admin does: `devices add` (prints a pairing string to paste into the new device), `remove`, `role`, `make-admin` and `replace-phrase` (phrase prompts hide the typing); removing a device from the keeper now moves the library to a new epoch, as in the app.
- Phones can add photos by hand (＋ in the Library): from any album, or from files (a file from a cloud drive or a USB
  stick is copied in, since the phone has no other copy).
- Devices → Storage shows how much of the library each device holds (originals, previews).
- Fixed: devices could show each other as offline for good while still syncing (after a phone slept or changed
  networks, or a keeper restarted); presence now reconnects by itself. The keeper lists devices by the names they
  report, as the app does. Setting up on a phone says "phone", not "computer".
- Checked on real devices: a Mac, a Samsung S25 on mobile data and a keeper on an x86_64 Linux box reach each other
  directly across networks.
- Shared albums belong to you, not to one device: join or create one on any device and all your devices take part;
  people see you once, under the name you set in *Settings → You* (the same on all your devices); leaving on one
  device leaves on all. You can share an album you already have (*Share this album*), and photos you add to it later
  are shared too.
- The Info panel's *Stored on* list uses the names devices report, like the Devices screen.
