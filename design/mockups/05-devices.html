05 · Devices & sync

In a peer-to-peer app the devices are the cloud, so they get a first-class tab: who's online, how they're connected, what each one keeps, and what's moving right now.

Mobile background sync

Best-effort (decision #10). The OS decides when the app runs in the background, so the app asks the user to open it when there's real work waiting and a peer is reachable. It never nags when nothing is waiting.

Headless keeper

The keeper has no camera or screen, so pairing runs the other way round from 5b. The phone shows a ticket string; the keeper's CLI dials it; both sides show the same code; the user confirms on the phone.
