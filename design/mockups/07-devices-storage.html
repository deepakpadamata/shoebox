What changes

  • The Storage list is gone; each device row carries its own bar and numbers, on one shared scale.
  • Make admin / Role / Remove move off the row: a ⋯ menu on desktop, a sheet when you tap a device on the phone. The rows stop being a wall of orange links.
  • A card at the top says what's moving, for how long, and opens into this device's queue. When nothing moves it shrinks to one green line, "Everything is backed up".
  • "Right now" on a row: what that device is getting or sending, and from whom.

Where the numbers come from

  • Storage per device, connection, latency: exist today (device list, holdings, presence).
  • "Originals waiting" on this device: exists (the fetch queue).
  • Time left: the at-risk rate the Storage screen already estimates with.
  • New (dashed outline): what's in flight with byte progress, transfer speed, and what this device is sending. The fetcher and the blob server have to start counting these.
  • New: "nest is getting from S25" on the phone is inferred from nest's holdings rising, not measured on nest.

States

  • Backing up: spinner, overall bar, time left.
  • Paused: amber, with the reason ("on mobile data", "battery low", "nest offline") and no bar.
  • All done: one green line; the queue is empty and hidden.
  • Light and dark both use the app's own tokens.
