Timeline states

Days with only a few photos share a row ("Mon 3 · Tue 4 · Wed 5"), so a quiet week no longer takes up a whole screen. Pinching out past the smallest density switches to month headers, with the date rail on the right. The status chip shows the state. If something is wrong, a banner explains it once, in one sentence, and offers one action.

Selection. Every action has a label. Share is first, under the thumb. Delete is last and red. Rare actions go under More: Keep on this phone, Remove from album, Copy to…. The bar replaces the tab bar so there is only one bottom layer.

Selection on desktop

On desktop the action bar sits in the toolbar, where macOS users look for it, and every action is also in the menu bar and the right-click menu. Shortcuts: ⌘A selects all, ⇧-click selects a range, Space opens Quick Look, ⌫ deletes, with a confirmation that names the number of photos and devices.
