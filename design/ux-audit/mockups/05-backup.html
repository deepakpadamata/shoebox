5 · Backup: one answer to "are my photos safe?"

Keeping photos safe is the whole promise of Shoebox, yet today the answer is spread over four places that disagree (F1, F15). The redesign merges Devices and Storage into one Backup page. On the phone it is the first section of the Settings tab, and the status chip on Photos opens it directly. On the Mac it is a sidebar row. It opens with a single sentence, worked out by one rule and used everywhere: the Photos chip, the Settings tab, the sidebar row, the viewer's Info and the banners.

The rule (one function, used everywhere)

This keeps the rule the core already uses (LibraryState::at_risk, and backed_up in core.rs): a photo is safe when its original is on at least copies to keep devices or on any keeper. What changes is that every screen reads that one result and describes it honestly. "Every photo has at least 2 copies" is never said when the real reason is "a keeper has it", and the device count is always stated. A library whose only device is one keeper gets its own neutral state. The words are fixed, in this order of precedence:

At risk

"1,203 photos are only on this phone."
Not safe by the rule (too few copies and no keeper has it), and no device that could take it has been seen in 7 days. Red. The Settings tab gets a dot and Photos shows one banner.

Waiting

"214 new photos are waiting for your Mac."
Not safe yet, but a known device will take them when it comes online. Amber. Says who and since when.

Backing up 23

"Backing up 23 photos to Mac Studio."
Transfers are running right now. Blue, with a count. Never "Syncing…" with no detail.

All backed up

"All 12,431 photos are on 2 devices."
Green and quiet. When there is only one device: "All on this Mac. Add a second device for a backup." This is neutral, not green, and never says "at least 2 copies".

Phone: the Backup page

Reached in one tap from the Photos status chip, or from the Backup card at the top of the Settings tab (see Settings). Day to day, the chip is all most people look at. This page is for when they want to know more, or when the chip turns amber or red.

Phone: device detail and add a device

Device actions are rare and some can't be undone, so they live one level down and only show when they apply. "Make admin" is hidden on an admin (F22). Roles are explained in what-it-does language ("Keeps every original" rather than "Keeper"), and the role word appears only as a small label.
