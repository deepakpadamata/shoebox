6 · Settings

Settings is the fourth phone tab, so it is always one tap away, and Backup is its first section. The everyday safety signal stays on Photos (the status chip), so the Settings tab is where you go to look closer or change something. Today Settings is one page with 14 sections of equal weight, 4½ phone screens long, with desktop-only path fields on the phone. The redesign puts Backup first, then seven groups you drill into, with rare or risky options under Advanced. Controls that can't work on a platform are not shown there. Fixes F4, F8, F9, F16, F24.

Desktop: two-pane settings

On the Mac, the same seven groups appear as a sidebar in a Settings window (⌘,), as in System Settings. The 520 px single column becomes a full-width two-pane layout. A search field filters settings by keyword. Watched folders, Export and Mac Photos are desktop-only and appear only here.
