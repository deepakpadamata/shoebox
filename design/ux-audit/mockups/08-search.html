Behaviour changes

  1. Phone: call blur() on submit, and on scroll start.
  2. Desktop: clear the toolbar query when the route leaves #/search. Esc in the field clears it and goes back.
  3. Coverage line ("Search covers 8,210 of 12,431 so far") only shows while indexing is still going.
  4. No results: suggest removing the most restrictive chip ("Try without 2026").
  5. ⌘F and / focus the desktop field. A ⌘K-style quick open could later also jump to albums and settings.
