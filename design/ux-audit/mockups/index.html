Shoebox redesign mockups

These are static mockups for the UX overhaul proposed in the report. Photos are generated gradients, and the device names and counts are made up. Colours, radii and type follow apps/desktop-mobile/ui/src/app.css, and the pages follow the system light or dark theme. Finding numbers (F1–F26) refer to findings.md.

The redesign in one picture

Four phone tabs, ordered by how often each job comes up. One safety answer, shown in the same words everywhere. Sharing is a property of an album. Settings are grouped, with the rare options under Advanced.

Photos · Albums · Search · Settings. No avatar menu. Favorites and People are on Albums. Backup (devices and storage) leads the Settings tab, and the Photos chip opens it.
