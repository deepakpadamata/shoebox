# M5-06 · Album activity feed, presence and profiles

## Why

Mockup 4b: 'who added what' is the main thing people want from a family album.

## Scope

- Events on add/remove/join/leave, grouped into feed entries.
- Per-album gossip topic: online members, 'new items' nudges.
- Display names and avatars shared with album members (avatar as a small blob).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Feed groups 12 adds by one member within 10 minutes into one entry.
- [ ] A name change propagates to album members within 1 minute online.
