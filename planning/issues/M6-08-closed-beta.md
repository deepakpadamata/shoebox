# M6-08 · Closed beta

## Why

Validate with 10–20 real households before release.

## Scope

- Recruit testers (mix of iOS/Android/desktop, at least 5 with a keeper).
- Feedback channel, triage labels, a weekly build cadence.
- Beta exit criteria: no data-loss bugs open, P1s fixed, at least 80% of households report 'backed up' within 24 h of install.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Beta report written with metrics and the decision to release (or not).

## Notes

The estimate covers setup and triage overhead. The beta itself runs 3–4 weeks of calendar time, alongside M7 prep.
