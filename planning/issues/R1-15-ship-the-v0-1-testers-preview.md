# R1-15 · Ship the v0.1 testers' preview

## Why

Put the app in the hands of a few people who will tell us what breaks: the first wave of the closed beta (M6-08).

## Scope

- Tag `v0.1.0`, release notes from the changelog.
- A one-page tester guide: what to install where, what to expect, known limits (no iOS, about 500–700 MB of memory per 100k
  photos on a phone, HEIC not imported, how to report a problem with a diagnostics report).
- **Owner:** pick 3–5 testers (at least one with a keeper) and a feedback channel.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] v0.1.0 artifacts published from the tag.
- [ ] Testers have installed it and the feedback channel has its first report.
