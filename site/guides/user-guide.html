Shoebox user guide

Shoebox keeps your photos on your devices and keeps them in sync directly between those devices. There is no account and no company server holding your library.

The ideas worth knowing

Your devices are your account. A "library" is the set of devices you have joined together. Whichever device you start the library on is the first member; you add others by scanning a code. If you lose a device, you remove it from another one.

Roles decide what each device keeps.

RoleKeepsGood for
Keeperevery original, alwaysa computer or small always-on box (Raspberry Pi, NAS, a cheap server)
Companionpreviews and thumbnails of everything, plus the full-size photos you pin, favourite (optional) or that were taken in the last 90 days (adjustable); older ones are fetched from a keeper when you open themphones, tablets and laptops
Viewerpreviews only, never originalsa family tablet

A role is not forever: Devices → Role next to a device changes it, for example when an old laptop becomes the keeper. It takes an admin device (the one you created the library on, and any device restored with the recovery phrase), or your recovery phrase from any other device. Devices → Make admin (with the phrase) gives you a second admin, so losing one device does not leave every change needing the phrase. The device switches as soon as it hears about the change.

Copy count is the number to watch. Every photo shows how many devices hold its original. The goal is two or more (you can change it in Settings), or one copy on a keeper: a keeper is the backstop, so a photo it holds counts as backed up. A photo only on a phone is one lost phone away from gone; the app tells you how many photos are in that state and when they will be safe.

Photos are only ever deleted by you. Delete moves a photo to Recently Deleted for 30 days on every device. Freeing space on a Companion removes only originals that a keeper, or at least two other devices, confirmed holding in the last week: the same rule as "backed up". Photos from the phone's own gallery are not freed this way: Shoebox keeps no copy of them, only the gallery does. Free-up also leaves alone what the Companion keeps by its own rules: recent photos, favourites (if kept) and pins.

What a Companion keeps is yours to set in Settings → What this device keeps: the recent window (off, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year), and whether favourites are kept. Pins are always kept. Changing the window changes what is fetched; nothing is removed until you choose Free up space.

First run

  1. Create a library on your first device. Give it a name and a role.
  2. Save the recovery kit. You are shown 24 words (and a printable page). They are the only way back if every device is lost. Store them somewhere safe and offline, then confirm. A banner reminds you until you do.
  3. Add a second device. On the first device open Devices → Add a device, choose its role and show the code. On the new device choose Add this computer to an existing library, scan or paste the code, then check that both screens show the same six digits and tap They match. If the digits differ, tap They do not match: someone is in the middle.
  4. Bring photos in. On a computer: Import a folder (the app can also watch a folder). On a phone: turn on camera backup in Settings; it uploads new photos to your other devices when conditions allow (Wi-Fi only, charging only and battery limits are yours to choose). Shoebox reads gallery photos where they are and keeps no second copy on the phone; delete a photo in the gallery and it is deleted from your library too (Recently Deleted, on every device). To add photos the backup does not see (albums you have not shared, a cloud drive), tap ＋ in the Library.

Sharing an album

Shared lists the albums you share with other people (who may be on their own libraries).

  • Your name. People you share with see the name in Settings → You, the same on all your devices (until you set one, they see the device's name).
  • All your devices take part. Join or create a shared album on one device and your other devices join it by themselves; the other people see you once, not once per device. Leaving on one device leaves on all of them.
  • Share an album you already have: open it under Albums and choose Share this album. Its photos are shared, and photos you add to the album later are shared too. (Removing a photo from your album does not remove it from the shared one.)
  • Create a shared album, then Invite: choose whether the person can add photos or only look. You get a QR code and a text code; the person opens Shared → Join with an invitation and scans or pastes it. Both of you need to be online while they join. An invitation works once and for seven days.
  • Adding photos. If a photo has a location, you are asked to keep or remove it before everyone sees where it was taken, and you can remember the choice for the album. Removing the location changes only the shared copy; your own photo keeps it. Some formats (such as HEIC) cannot be cleaned yet and are left out.
  • Save to my library copies a shared photo into yours.
  • Remove someone (owners only, in the People tab): the album moves to a new private space and everyone else follows automatically. The person removed keeps the photos they already downloaded: that cannot be undone.
  • Leave an album from its settings. You keep anything you saved to your library.

New versions. The app tells you when a newer Shoebox is out (a banner with a download link); install it over the old one and your library stays. It asks the Shoebox website twice a day and sends nothing about you; turn it off in Settings → About. A keeper mentions new versions in shoebox-keeper status.

When something goes wrong

  • A device was lost or stolen. On another device: Devices → Remove next to it. The remaining devices move to a new library space the lost device cannot read. If the lost device was an admin (the one you created the library on is), or you remove it from a device that is not one, you are asked for your 24-word recovery phrase: that way a thief holding an admin device cannot remove you instead.
  • All devices were lost. Install Shoebox, choose I lost my devices, enter the 24 words and the link of any device that still has the library. (A keeper prints its link with shoebox-keeper address.) If that device has a screen, it asks whether to let the new device in: allow it there within two minutes.
  • Someone may have seen your recovery phrase. Settings → Replace recovery phrase, enter the current one, and save the new kit it shows (once). Your devices switch to the new phrase as soon as they are in touch, and the old one opens nothing. Remove any device you do not recognise first.
  • "Your library's recovery phrase was replaced". Shown on your other devices after a replacement. If it was you, get the new phrase to wherever you keep it; if not, check Devices for devices you do not know.
  • "… became an admin of your library". Shown on every device when a new admin appears, for example when someone restores with the recovery phrase. If it was not you, someone has your phrase: remove that device (Devices → Remove, with the phrase) and keep the phrase somewhere safer.
  • Photos show "not backed up". You need another device online. Add a keeper, or open the app on your laptop while it is on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Ask for help. Settings → Help → Create a diagnostics report makes a text with versions, counts and recent errors. It contains no photos, names, places or file names. Nothing is sent: copy it or save it.

Your data is yours

Settings → Export copies every original on that device into plain folders by year and month with a library.json for favorites and albums. If you stop using Shoebox you lose nothing.

Privacy in one paragraph

Devices connect directly to each other, end-to-end encrypted. When two devices cannot reach each other directly they go through a relay that only forwards encrypted bytes; the relay can see that two devices talk and how much, not what. You can run your own relay (relay-guide.md). Shoebox collects no analytics and has no accounts.
