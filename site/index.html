Your photos, on your own devices.

Shoebox syncs your library directly between your phone, your computer and a small always-on box at home. There is no account to create and no company holding your pictures.

How it works

Devices are the account

Add a device by scanning a code and comparing six digits. Lose one, and you remove it from another.

A keeper makes it safe

A Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a spare computer holds every original. Your phone keeps previews and frees space safely.

Copy count, not guesswork

Every photo shows how many devices hold it. The app tells you what is not backed up yet and when it will be.

Share without a cloud

Shared albums travel between households. Take the location off a photo before it leaves, and remove a person later if you need to.

Your files stay yours

Export everything as plain folders at any time. Nothing is locked in.

Open source

Built on iroh peer-to-peer connections. Read the code and the threat model.

Privacy

Devices connect directly and everything is encrypted end to end. When two devices cannot reach each other the traffic goes through a relay that only forwards encrypted bytes; you can run your own. Shoebox has no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. The app's help report contains counts and versions only: no photos, names, places or file names, and it is never sent unless you send it.
